Haunt

A small ghost that haunts your screen until it can see your task is done. Its voice and its eyes are a local Gemma model, so your screen never leaves your laptop.

How it works

  1. Add a task. Every few minutes the ghost fades in over whatever you are doing, with a line from Gemma. The longer you ignore it, the angrier it gets.
  2. Click I did it. The ghost hides, takes one screenshot, and asks Gemma whether it shows the work.
  3. If it does not, the ghost tells you what it does see, and stays.

I see a YouTube video of cats. That is not "Finish the report".

Tasks tied to a file or a git commit finish on their own the moment you save or commit, with no AI involved.

Why a local model

A ghost that watches your desktop sees your mail and your messages. That is only acceptable if the screenshot goes to your own machine and nowhere else.
